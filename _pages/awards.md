This page summarizes scholarships, academic awards, and other recognitions I have received during my undergraduate studies in computer science.

### Harpp Undergraduate Award
Awarded for participation in the Faculty of Science Peer Mentoring Program for COMP 250 during the Winter 2026 session. Peer tutors are selected by the course instructors based on academic achievement and teaching potential.

### [Dean's Honour List (2024-2025)](https://www.mcgill.ca/studentaid/scholarships-aid/current-undergrads/merit)
Awarded for academic excellence to the top 10% of continuing students in the Faculty of Science based on the combined GPA for the Fall and Winter terms.

### Mr. Chan Chee Keung and Mrs. Chan Au Yuk Bing Memorial Scholarship
Awarded by the Faculty of Science Scholarships Committee on the basis of academic merit for one or more outstanding students who have completed at least one year in a BSc program in the Faculty of Science.

### Richard Tomlinson Undergraduate Award
Awarded for participation in the Faculty of Science Peer Mentoring Program for COMP 251 during the Fall 2025 session. Peer tutors are selected by the course instructors based on academic achievement and teaching potential.

### [Science Undergraduate Research Award 2025](https://www.mcgill.ca/science/research/undergraduate-research/sura)
Awared a research grant to engage in 15 weeks of full-time research and development activity under the supervision of a McGill science professor. SURA recipients are selected on the basis of academic excellence, research potential and other factors.

### Tomlinson Engagement Award for Mentoring
Awarded twice for performing tutoring responsibilities and hosting mid term review sessions as part of being a CSUS Helpdesk Tutor.
